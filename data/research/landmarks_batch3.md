# Landmark research — batch 3 (`viewer/src/landmarks/batch3.ts`)

Companion to `landmarks.md` (batches 1–2) and `landmark_audit.md` (orientation/placement audit, Nov 2026).
Rows for every model below were appended to `data/tables/landmarks.csv`.

## Method
- **Footprints**: OpenStreetMap outlines via Overture 2026-10-21.0 (`data/raw/overture/2026-10-21.0/building*.parquet`;
  © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). For each building the OSM centroid is the anchor and the outline is
  simplified (0.5–1.2 m) in a frame aligned with the building (`frame(0,0,0,bearing)`: +y at `bearing`, +x at
  `bearing + 90°`; the north-of-Market grid is 171°/81°, SoMa 135°/45°, Treasure Island 152°/62°). Where OSM has 3D
  parts (Mark Hopkins, Russ, 450 Sutter, 555 California, St. Mary's, SFMOMA) each part is placed at its own centroid.
- **Placement check**: every model's top view was overlaid on the OSM outlines and on an orthophoto (Esri World
  Imagery, viewed only) — see `landmark_audit.md` for the tooling.
- **Heights**: Wikipedia infoboxes / articles (roof or architectural height) or the OSM `height` tag, as named in
  each model's facts. Setbacks, crowns, roof pitches and colours come from the reference photos (listed per item);
  anything estimated is named in the model's `Model:` fact.
- **Photos**: Wikimedia Commons, 960 px thumbnails, with `_meta.jsonl` (page, author, licence) per folder under
  `data/raw/reference_photos/`. Wikimedia rate-limited this session repeatedly; a few items have no photo (noted).

## Derivations worth knowing
- **Tower of Jewels (PPIE 1915)** — primary source: John D. Barry, *The City of Domes* (1915, public domain,
  Project Gutenberg #3151): "Main tower breaking southern wall, facing South Gardens … 433 feet high … seven
  stages"; "Arch of Tower of Jewels, 110 feet high, 60 feet broad"; Court of the Universe "650 feet wide from arch
  to arch; 1200 feet from Tower of Jewels to Column of Progress"; palaces around the court NE Transportation, NW
  Agriculture, SW Liberal Arts, SE Manufactures; Arches of the Rising/Setting Sun 203 ft; Palace of Horticulture dome
  186 ft high, 152 ft diameter; Machinery Palace "three blocks long, seven acres". Official height 435 ft (Wikipedia).
  Position: the walled palace group ran from the Fine Arts lagoon (Baker St) to the Machinery Palace (east of
  Fillmore St); its centre is on the Scott St axis; 1,200 ft south of a bay-front Column of Progress puts the tower at
  ≈ Scott & Bay St (DataSF street centrelines) — **±50 m, LOW CONFIDENCE**. The other palaces were not modelled:
  no georeferenced site plan was reachable from this session (the 1938 Ryker mosaic / Stanford WMS host was
  blocked); a traced PPIE plan would allow the full walled city.
- **Midwinter Fair (1894)** — Wikipedia (California Midwinter International Exposition): Bonet Electric Tower 266 ft
  in the centre of the Grand Court of Honor (= today's Music Concourse), stood about two years; Administration
  Building (A. Page Brown) at the west end where the Temple of Music stands, 135 ft dome; Mechanical Arts Building
  (Edward Swain) 300 × 160 ft where the Academy of Sciences stands. The tower is placed on the concourse's central
  fountain (orthophoto); hall footprints/roofs estimated.
- **Montgomery Block (1853–1959)** — SE corner of Montgomery & Washington, frontage Washington → Merchant St
  (DataSF centrelines: ≈ 37 m between kerbs); depth ≈ 42 m and light court estimated; 4 storeys, 15 m (Wikipedia).
  HABS CA-1228 (https://www.loc.gov/item/ca0675/) has measured drawings — the next step for a better footprint.
- **Fleishhacker Pool** — 1,000 × 150 ft (Wikipedia); placed on the zoo car park, the only clearing of that size
  (orthophoto), aligned N–S; the bathhouse position is a guess from the 1930s HABS aerial (LOW CONFIDENCE).
- **Hyatt Regency** — the stepped south-east face is generated by clipping the OSM outline with a half-plane that
  recedes 55 m toward the NW corner over the 77 m height (slope estimated from the plaza photo).
- **St. Mary's Cathedral** — eight bilinear (hyperbolic-paraboloid) patches between corner pylon, side mid-point
  slot, slot top and apex; square (63 m) and top cross from the OSM 3D parts; 190 ft shell and the cross height
  (77.7 m OSM) documented; pylon and slot heights estimated from the photo.
- **Not modelled** (could not document well enough this session): Tower of the Sun (GGIE 1939, 400 ft — no
  georeferenced plan of the fair; Buildings 1–3 on Treasure Island are modelled instead), the PPIE palaces, Playland,
  Hobart Building and Hallidie Building (photos rate-limited), Ghirardelli clock tower, Lombard St, Pier 39,
  Exploratorium, Moscone Center, One Rincon Hill, Hunters Point crane, Octagon House, Spreckels Mansion.
